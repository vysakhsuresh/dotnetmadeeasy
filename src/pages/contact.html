What to contact us about

A mistake in a lesson

Tell us the lesson name and what is wrong — a sample that does not compile, an explanation that is inaccurate or unclear, a broken link. Please include the .NET version you are on if the problem is a compile error. We fix confirmed mistakes directly in the lesson.

A topic you want covered

If you searched the course for something and did not find it, we want to know. The most useful suggestions describe the situation you were in ("I was trying to…") rather than just naming a feature.

Something that is not working on the site

A lesson that will not load, progress that did not save, a layout problem on your device — include your browser and device and, if you can, a screenshot.

Custom .NET engineering work

Layerbit, the studio behind this course, takes on .NET development, architecture reviews and production troubleshooting for clients. If you have a project, email us with a short description and we will get back to you.

Response times

We are a small team in the United Arab Emirates. Email is usually answered within two working days; WhatsApp faster during UAE working hours. We do not offer live tutoring or homework help, but a question that reveals a gap in a lesson will usually get the lesson improved.

Prefer not to write? Every lesson has a "Was this lesson helpful?" control at the bottom. It is anonymous and it does reach us.
