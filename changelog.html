Changelog

What’s new

Every change to the course, newest first. .NET ships a new version every November, and the lessons move with it: the .NET 11 update went live the day after its release.

Every lesson shows the date it was last updated. To follow changes as they happen, add the feed of new and updated lessons to any feed reader. Spotted something out of date? Tell us and it gets fixed.

  1. 11 November 2026

    Updated for .NET 11 and C# 15

    .NET 11 and C# 15 were released on 10 November 2026. The course was updated the next day.

    • New Part XIII, What's New in .NET 11 and C# 15: ten beginner-friendly lessons on union types, closed hierarchies, collection expression arguments, extension indexers, labeled break and continue, runtime async, the new library APIs, ASP.NET Core 11 and EF Core 11.
    • A step-by-step checklist for upgrading a real project to .NET 11.
    • The setup and first-program lessons now install .NET 11 and target net11.0, with a note on .NET 10 as the long-term support release.
    • The cheat sheet, glossary and interview guide gained C# 15 syntax, terms and questions, and the file-based apps lesson covers what .NET 11 adds.
  2. 18 October 2026

    A .NET developer roadmap and faster pages

    A new planning guide, and a pass over speed and accessibility across the whole site.

    • New: the .NET developer roadmap, a stage-by-stage plan from your first program to production, with every stage linked to its lessons.
    • Pages load noticeably faster, and contrast, keyboard navigation and screen-reader support were improved everywhere.
    • Every lesson now shows the date it was last updated, and an RSS feed lists new and updated lessons.
  3. 17 October 2026

    Quizzes, progress and reading time

    Tools that help you learn, not just read.

    • Interactive quizzes: choose an answer and see straight away why it is right or wrong.
    • Reading time on every lesson, progress for each part, and a daily learning streak.
    • Code examples repaired in 163 lessons, and the mobile layout tidied.
  4. 27 September 2026

    A new design and four guide pages

    The course player was redesigned and the site gained its first guide pages.

    • Larger, easier-to-read type and a calmer palette in the course player.
    • New guides: Start Here, Interview Prep, the C# cheat sheet and the .NET glossary.
    • Every lesson page gained the full site navigation, so any lesson can be opened, bookmarked and shared on its own.
  5. 6 September 2026

    Reader mode and Listen to this lesson

    More comfortable ways to work through long lessons.

    • Adjustable text size, a distraction-free focus mode and a reading progress bar.
    • Listen to this lesson: your browser reads the lesson aloud.
  6. 1 September 2026

    The course is complete

    The Production .NET tier was finished, completing all three tiers, and the course moved to dotnetmadeeasy.com.

    • Production .NET: thirteen parts, from runtime internals and async to distributed systems, cloud, testing, modern C# 14 and the OrderFlow capstone.
    • Full-text search across every lesson, related-lesson suggestions and a “Was this lesson helpful?” control on each lesson.
  7. 30 August 2026

    C# In Practice tier complete

    The middle tier, for developers who know the basics, was published in full.

    • All 90 C# In Practice lessons: generics, LINQ, delegates and events, async, dependency injection, HTTP and data access, ending with complete projects.
    • C# Foundations Parts IV to VII: collections through practical development.
  8. 15 August 2026

    The course begins

    The first C# Foundations lessons were published, starting from what C# and .NET are and how to set up your machine.
